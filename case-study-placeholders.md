## 3. Rendevor

**Case Study Title:** Standardizing Audits Across 70+ Facilities with AI

**Description:** Standardized a black-box audit process across 70+ dialysis facilities nationwide, some restricted to paper-only workflows, into a single system that works whether an audit happens on a device, in Excel, or on paper.
